Synthesized instruments

Vintage E-Piano, Synth, Warm Synth Pad, Poly Synth, Saw Synth, Synth Brass, Funky Worm, G-Funk Whistle, Funk Organ and Clavinet are synthesized by Song Rider Suite itself.

Backup sounds

If a recording cannot load, the app falls back to the General MIDI sounds from MIDI.js Soundfonts by Benjamin Gleitzman (MusyngKite and FluidR3_GM), used under their authors' licenses.
